About PlagiaScan AI Lite

AI-based plagiarism detection built for academic integrity

PlagiaScan AI Lite is a lightweight, self-hostable plagiarism detection platform that combines classical NLP techniques with modern semantic similarity to help students, teachers and institutions verify document authenticity.

It uses TF-IDF vectorisation, cosine similarity, sentence transformer embeddings, spaCy for linguistic processing and NLTK for tokenisation to compare an uploaded document against every other document stored in the system and produce a detailed similarity report.

Beyond matching, the engine estimates the probability that text was AI-generated versus human-written using structural heuristics, and assigns a confidence score so users understand the reliability of each result.

Important: PlagiaScan AI Lite provides probabilistic analysis only. It does not verify legal copyright ownership, and its AI-detection estimate is not a definitive verdict.
